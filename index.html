01Why it matters

Residential proxy exits churn faster than detection feeds update.

A third of residential proxy exit IPs last a single day (IPinfo / AbuseIPDB, 2026), but the major detection feeds update daily or within 24–48 hours. proxywatch is built to catch them within minutes, so you can challenge or block those logins.

02Who it's for

Banks, fintechs, and any company where a bad login is expensive. Inside them, the fraud team that owns login policy.

The login they can't decide on

An account usually logs in from California. A new login arrives, also from California, but from a new IP address, a slightly different location, maybe a different telecom provider. It could be the customer, so there isn't enough information to block or challenge it. Residential proxies are chosen to look exactly like this. But if you knew the IP was a residential proxy exit right now, you could challenge or block the login. That's the whole point of the product.

Why they'd switch

  • Fresher data: exits flagged in minutes, not days.
  • Test it free on your own production traffic for a day or two, and see for yourself.
  • Very easy to sign up: get a key without leaving the terminal, no sales call.
  • Cheaper, with no vendor lock-in.

03How it works

1 · Probe

Send tiny requests through residential proxy networks, around the clock, to our own server.

2 · Record

Each arrival reveals an exit IP. Store when it was an exit, to the minute.

3 · Look up

One API call: is this IP a proxy exit now, or was it at a given time?

4 · Act

Fresh exit + new IP for this account → challenge or block.

US exits only, to start: they're the most valuable. Probes run only through vetted providers that verify their customers and claim consent from device owners.

04Focus: Systems

Speed is a systems property. The product is the pipeline: probes running around the clock, storage that can answer "was this IP an exit at 2:14 PM?", a fast lookup API, and usage metering behind terminal signup.

We'll know it works by measuring time-to-flag: for every exit we see, how many minutes until proxywatch flags it, and whether the incumbents have flagged it 5 minutes, 1 hour, and 24 hours later.
